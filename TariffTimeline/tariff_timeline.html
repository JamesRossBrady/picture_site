How the "reciprocal" rate was actually calculated ↗ click for USTR source
$$\Delta\tau_i = \frac{x_i - m_i}{\varepsilon \cdot \phi \cdot m_i}$$
where \(x_i\) = U.S. exports to country \(i\), \(m_i\) = U.S. imports from it, and the elasticities were set to \(\varepsilon = 4\), \(\phi = 0.25\), so \(\varepsilon\phi = 1\).
$$\Rightarrow \Delta\tau_i = \frac{x_i - m_i}{m_i} = \frac{\text{trade deficit}}{\text{imports}}$$
$$\text{Imposed "reciprocal" tariff}_i = \tfrac{1}{2}\,\Delta\tau_i \quad \text{(halved, "being kind")}$$
